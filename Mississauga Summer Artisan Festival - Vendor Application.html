Terms & Conditions

Vendor Eligibility

  • Food vendors must accurately describe all products intended for sale.
  • Only approved food items may be sold at the event.
  • Vendors must disclose all menu items and categories in their application.

Booth Space & Vendor Responsibilities

  • Each vendor receives an assigned event space.
  • Vendors are responsible for bringing all equipment needed including tents, tables, generators, signage and display materials.
  • Tent vendors must bring their own setup equipment.
  • Vendors must remain within assigned space limits at all times.

Health & Safety

  • Vendors are responsible for complying with all applicable health regulations.
  • Vendors are responsible for securing all required permits, licenses, and insurance.

Cleanup

  • Vendors are responsible for garbage removal and keeping booth spaces clean throughout the event.

Payment & Acceptance

  • Vendor Fee: $175 + HST
  • Submission does not guarantee acceptance.
  • Space is confirmed only after approval and full payment.
  • Vendor fees are non-refundable.
  • The event proceeds rain or shine.
  • For questions or assistance, please contact: art@saugaartisanfest.ca

Marketing

  • By applying, vendors grant permission for Sauga Artisan Festival to use submitted photos and videos for marketing purposes.

Conduct

  • Sauga Artisan Festival maintains a respectful and inclusive environment.
  • Harassment or abusive behaviour will not be tolerated and may result in removal from the event.
